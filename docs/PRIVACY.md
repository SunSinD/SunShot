# Privacy

Screen capture, editing, clipboard actions, and local history run on your computer. SunShot has no advertising or analytics service.

Uploads send the selected content and any necessary credentials to the destination you configure. Workflows, hotkeys, and watch folders can perform uploads when enabled. New profiles do not upload captures automatically. Review imported settings before enabling uploads or shortcuts.

Automatic update checks are off by default. A manual update check contacts GitHub for SunShot releases. Recording offers to download FFmpeg from ShareX's GitHub release on first use; its checksum is verified before installation in your SunShot profile. Downloads and online tools contact their respective providers. Some provider login flows still use ShareX's registered callback page because those addresses belong to the providers' application registrations.

Settings, backups, history, and logs are stored in your SunShot profile. Saved destination credentials may be included in settings or backups. Do not publish your profile or attach it to a bug report without checking its contents.
