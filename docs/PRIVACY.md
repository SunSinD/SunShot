# Privacy

Captures, editing, clipboard actions and history stay on your computer unless you choose an upload or online tool. SunShot has no ads or analytics.

Uploads send selected content and required credentials to the destination you configure. New profiles do not upload captures automatically. Review imported tasks before enabling uploads or hotkeys.

Automatic update checks are off by default. Manual checks contact GitHub. Recording can download FFmpeg from ShareX's GitHub release and verifies its checksum first.

Settings, history, backups and logs are stored in the SunShot profile. They may contain saved credentials. Check the profile before sharing it.
